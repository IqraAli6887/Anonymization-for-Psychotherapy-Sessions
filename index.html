The whole method

Three parts, then release. The de-identified text is written by a human rewriter and checked by a second person; the paraphrased text is written by an Agent and approved or corrected by a BIU reviewer. There is no separate clinician approver. Click a role to see what it does.

Agent: finds, suggests and checks Human: decides and writes

    Sequential dependency

    Turns are never split, merged, moved or deleted, so dependencies survive. The Sequential Dependency Agent proposes links; a BIU reviewer confirms them at Checkpoint B. Click a turn, a span or an arrow.

    Client Therapist Solid arrow: confirmed at Checkpoint B Dotted arrow: unresolved, never a gold label ✕ Rejected

    Longitudinal handling

    Tracks are keyed to a MIND element and an entity ID, not to surface words, so they follow the same person across sessions after de-identification. Click a dot, the red cross or the change-log bar.

    Paraphrasing

    QMUL’s paraphrasing Agent rewords each de-identified turn in session order. Automatic checks send failures back up to three times; a BIU reviewer then approves or corrects every turn. Click any box.
